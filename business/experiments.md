# Experiments

## E001 — Free mini-audit to founding offer

- Hypothesis: A specific, no-call three-point mini-audit will produce at least one positive reply from 20 carefully selected UAE service businesses.
- What is being tested: Personalized email with one observed conversion issue and a link to request the free mini-audit.
- Cost: AED 0.
- Expected result: 20 contacts → 3 replies → 2 mini-audits → 1 paid audit at AED 349.
- Actual result: 5 of 20 individualized emails sent on 2026-10-02; replies and conversions pending.
- What was learned: Pending.
- Decision: Iterate after 20 delivered emails or seven days, whichever comes first.

## E002 — Founding price

- Hypothesis: AED 349 is low enough for a first transaction and high enough to validate meaningful willingness to pay.
- What is being tested: Fixed transparent price, 24-hour delivery, and “fewer than three real issues means you pay nothing.”
- Cost: AED 0 before payment fees.
- Expected result: At least 25% of qualified mini-audit recipients request payment instructions.
- Actual result: Pending.
- What was learned: Pending.
- Decision: Hold price through five customers; do not discount further without evidence.
