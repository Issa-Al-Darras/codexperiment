# Business strategy

## Primary opportunity: LeakFix

LeakFix is a productized, human-reviewed website conversion teardown for UAE service businesses. The entry offer is a free three-point mini-audit. The paid offer costs AED 349 for the first five clients and includes a scorecard, five or more prioritized findings, three hero-copy directions, and one email follow-up, delivered within 24 hours.

### Why this won

It can earn revenue without inventory, ad spend, paid APIs, or a large audience. Delivery can be mostly automated while preserving a human-reviewed promise. Public competitors validate one-off pricing from US$49 to US$499, and the free mini-audit creates a low-friction way to test willingness to engage before any spend.

### Initial customer

UAE professional-service firms with a public English-language website and an enquiry-based funnel: corporate-services providers, consultancies, clinics, agencies, and home-service firms. The first niche test is Dubai corporate-services firms because one new client can be worth far more than the audit fee.

### Acquisition

1. Send five carefully researched, individually written emails per weekday; never bulk-send.
2. Each email includes one specific public-site observation and offers the free three-point audit.
3. Route prospects to the landing page or direct reply.
4. Ask interested prospects for the public URL and primary conversion goal.
5. Deliver the free mini-audit, then offer the full AED 349 teardown.

### Unit economics

- Revenue per founding audit: AED 349
- Cash cost per audit: AED 0 before payment fees
- Target delivery time: 60–90 minutes
- Gross margin before payment fees and owner labor: approximately 100%
- Break-even: first collected sale

### Constraints

No payment is accepted until the owner provides a lawful payment method or invoicing path. No testimonials or performance claims will be invented. Client analytics and credentials are not required.
